New Player Roadmap

Simplified Progression

A straight-line path from your first quest to endgame. Check steps off as you go; the goal is to spend resources only when they actually move you into the next useful breakpoint.

This roadmap is intentionally practical rather than exhaustive. If you are stuck, improve the systems named in your current phase before spending heavily on temporary gear.
Character Routine

Progression Checklist

Track the repeatable tasks that are easy to leave on the table. Daily and Weekly progress can be reset independently; pet collection ranks remain permanent and are shared with the Pet Atlas.

Checklist state is stored in this browser and included in progress exports. Daily/Weekly reset buttons only clear their own checklist progress; they never reset pet ranks.
